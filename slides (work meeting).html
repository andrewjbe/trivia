Part 1: Oklahoma — Answers

Question 1

What is the state bird of Oklahoma?

  • Answer: The scissor-tailed flycatcher

Question 2

In what decade did Oklahoma last have a Democrat for a Senator?

  • Answer: The 1990s
    • David Boren left the Senate in 1994 to become president of the University of Oklahoma, and it’s been all GOP since.

Question 3

In 2007, the illustrious Oklahoma state legislature determined that our state vegetable would be what?

  • Answer: Watermelon
    • You may notice that this is a fruit, not a vegetable. Welcome to Oklahoma!

Question 4

What NBA team moved to Oklahoma City in 2008 to become our beloved Thunder?


  • Answer: The Seattle SuperSonics

Question 5

Which has a greater population: the state of Oklahoma or the country of Denmark?

  • Answer: Denmark
    • Denmark has about 6 million people, compared to Oklahoma’s roughly 4.1 million.

Part 2: Fictional Countries — Answers

Question 6

In The Princess Diaries (2001), Anne Hathaway is whisked away to inherit the throne of what nonexistent European principality?

  • Answer: Genovia

Question 7

The city-state of Ankh-Morpork is the primary setting of what beloved fantasy series by Terry Pratchett?


  • Answer: Discworld

Question 8

Gulliver’s Travels took him to what country of tiny people?


  • Answer: Lilliput

Question 9

In Lord of the Rings, what country of Men is home to our friends Boromir and Faramir?


  • Answer: Gondor

Question 10

In the world of Avatar: The Last Airbender, everything changed when what nation attacked?


  • Answer: The Fire Nation

Part 3: Politics & History — Answers

Question 11

Which of Bill Clinton’s campaign strategists famously said “it’s the economy, stupid!”


  • Answer: James Carville

Question 12

What U.S. President declared “I’m not for women in any job. I don’t want any of them around. Thank God we don’t have any in the cabinet.”?

  • Answer: Richard Nixon

Question 13

Winston Churchill was the Prime Minister of the U.K. when Queen Elizabeth took the throne. Who was the Prime Minister when she died in 2022?

  • Answer: Liz Truss
    • She was in office for less than 50 days, but managed to become one of the most unpopular PMs in history.
    • In October of 2022 once poll recorded her approval rating at 9%. Yikes!

Question 14

The largest political party in the world by membership is located in what country?

  • Answer: India
    • The Indian People’s Party, or BJP, has over 100 million registered members!

Question 15

What governing document did the U.S. Constitution replace?


  • Answer: The Articles of Confederation
